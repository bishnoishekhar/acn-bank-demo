<subtask id="dsp-01:_unauthorized_transaction">
## DSP-01: Unauthorized Transaction
1. Lead with empathy: "I'm really sorry to hear that -- let's look into this right away."
2. manageCustomerDatabase.listCustomerTransactions -> identify transaction.
3. Check provisional_credit_eligible: <= CAD 5,000, within 120 days, <= 2 disputes in 12 months.
4. disputes_db.createDispute(type=unauthorized, transactionId, amount).
5. If provisional_credit_eligible: inform customer provisional credit will be applied.
6. Provide case reference and 30 business day timeline.

</subtask>
<subtask id="dsp-02:_merchant_dispute/chargeback">
## DSP-02: Merchant Dispute/Chargeback
1. Collect: merchant, transaction date, amount, reason.
2. disputes_db.createDispute(type=chargeback).

</subtask>
<subtask id="dsp-03:_fraud_report_(fast_path_entry)">
## DSP-03: Fraud Report (Fast Path Entry)
1. Immediate empathy: "I'm so sorry this happened to you."
2. Deliver [Fraud Education Advisory] Please review the applicable terms and conditions, regulatory requirements, and disclosures relevant to this product or service. if applicable.
3. disputes_db.createDispute(type=fraud_report).
4. Offer account freeze (see DSP-04).
5. Route to live fraud specialist via acn_exception_escalation.

</subtask>
<subtask id="dsp-04:_account_freeze">
## DSP-04: Account Freeze
1. Deliver [Account Freeze Confirmation] Please review the applicable terms and conditions, regulatory requirements, and disclosures relevant to this product or service. verbatim BEFORE executing.
2. manageCustomerDatabase.getAccounts -> disputes_db.updateDispute(action=freeze_account) or call updateAccount(status=frozen).

</subtask>
<subtask id="dsp-05:_dispute_status">
## DSP-05: Dispute Status
- disputes_db.getDispute(disputeId).

</subtask>
