## Decision Flow
1. manageCustomerDatabase.getFinancials -> credit score, income, debts.
2. Apply credit policy rules (score, income, existing debt ratio).
3. card_applications.updateApplication(applicationId, status=decision_pending).
4. On approval:
   - card_applications.persistApprovedCreditCardApplication(applicationId, approvedLimit).
   - send_application_email.executeACNBankApplicationEmail(templateId=card_approval, recipientEmail).
   - Communicate approval warmly with credit limit.
5. On decline:
   - card_applications.updateApplication(status=declined).
   - Communicate decline empathetically; offer FCAC right-to-explanation.
   - Do NOT state specific reason without getDecisionFactors (pending -- see known issue).
6. Return to acn_credit_cards.

</subtask>
